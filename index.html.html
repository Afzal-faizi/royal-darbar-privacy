Privacy Policy — Royal Darbar Ordering Assistant

Last updated: September 2026

Royal Darbar ("we", "our", "us") operates an automated WhatsApp ordering assistant to help customers place pickup food orders. This page explains what information we collect through that service and how it is used.

Information We Collect

When you place an order through our WhatsApp ordering assistant, we collect:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • The name you provide for the pickup order
  • The items, quantities, and any preferences included in your order
  • Message content you send to the assistant, for the purpose of processing your order

How We Use Your Information

We use this information solely to:

  • Process and prepare your pickup order
  • Contact you regarding your order if necessary
  • Maintain order records for our restaurant operations

Information We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes
  • We do not use your data for advertising

Third-Party Services

Our ordering assistant uses WhatsApp's Business API (provided by Meta) to send and receive messages, and Google's Gemini API to process and respond to order conversations. These providers may process message data as part of delivering their services to us, subject to their own privacy policies.

Data Retention

Order information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for restaurant record-keeping and customer service purposes.

Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we have on file for your phone number, or to request its deletion.
